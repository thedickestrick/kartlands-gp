How to play

The short version

Driving

Two players

Player 1WASD drive, Shift drifts, E item, F special Player 2Arrow keys drive, Space drifts, Enter item, / special

Controller

Either stick or the d-pad steers. A (cross) or the left stick forward accelerates; B (circle) or the stick back brakes. The bumpers drift. The right trigger (R2) uses the item from a crate and the left trigger (L2) fires your special weapon. Back resets, Start pauses. A second pad drives Player 2.

Staying in one piece

ArmorEvery hit costs armor, and at zero the vehicle is wrecked and rebuilt a moment later. It mends when you are left alone, and coins patch it up. Heavy vehicles carry more and spin for less.
Special weaponEach driver has one of their own. The meter fills with time, and faster with coins, tricks and hits.
DriftHold drift while turning and the sparks change colour: yellow, green, then pink. Let go for a boost that matches.
TricksHold drift as you leave a ramp. The kart rolls, and you get a boost when you land it.
CoinsEach coin adds a little top speed, up to ten. A hit scatters three; a fall costs two, a wreck four.
Star tokensThree on every circuit: one high over a jump, two out on the shoulders. Each is worth three coins, a boost and a patch of armor.
EdgesStriped barriers are safe to lean on. Where there is no barrier there is a drop, lava or water, and gaps in the road have to be jumped. Fall and you are put back a moment later.
