How to play

The short version

Driving

W ↑Accelerate S ↓Brake, reverse A D ← →Steer Shift SpaceDrift, and trick off a ramp E Ctrl EnterUse item RPut me back on the road EscPause MSound on or off

Two players

Player 1WASD drive, Shift drifts, E fires Player 2Arrow keys drive, Space drifts, Enter fires

Controller

Either stick or the d-pad steers. Right trigger or A accelerates, left trigger or B brakes, bumpers or X drift, Y fires, Back resets, Start pauses. A second pad drives Player 2.

Going faster

DriftHold drift while turning and the sparks change colour: yellow, green, then pink. Let go for a boost that matches.
TricksHold drift as you leave a ramp. The kart rolls, and you get a boost when you land it.
CoinsEach coin adds a little top speed, up to ten. Getting hit scatters three; falling off costs two.
Star tokensThree on every circuit: one high over a jump, two out on the shoulders. Each is worth three coins and a boost, and the game remembers the ones you have found.
Boost padsGreen chevrons on the road. Drive over them.
EdgesStriped barriers are safe to lean on. Where there is no barrier there is a drop, lava or water, and gaps in the road have to be jumped. Fall and you are put back a moment later.
